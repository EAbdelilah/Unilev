# ESWAP Protocol Specification: 0% Interest Margin on Uniswap V4

This document outlines the final technical architecture and business integration strategy for the ESWAP Protocol, implemented on Uniswap V4.

## 1. Technical Architecture

### 1.1 Transient Flash Borrowing (EIP-1153)
ESWAP eliminates external lending pools by utilizing the `PoolManager`'s own reserves.
*   **Mechanism:** During `beforeSwap`, the hook returns a negative `BeforeSwapDelta`. This effectively "borrows" the required capital from the V4 singleton for the duration of the swap execution.
*   **Scaling:** Leverage is not capped by an ESWAP-specific pool, but by the global liquidity depth of Uniswap V4 ($5B+).

### 1.2 Custom Accounting & Claim Tokens (ERC-6909)
*   **Hook-Held Collateral:** The hook intercepts the swap output using the `BEFORE_SWAP_RETURNS_DELTA_FLAG`.
*   **Settlement:** Instead of sending tokens to the trader, the hook "takes" the tokens from the `PoolManager` and maps them to the trader via internal ERC-6909 balances.
*   **Gas Efficiency:** By keeping assets within the Singleton and using internal accounting, transfer costs are reduced by ~60%.

### 1.3 The 0% Interest Engine (Smart Collateral)
*   **Rehypothecation:** 100% of the trader's collateral is automatically deployed as **concentrated liquidity** around the current price tick.
*   **Yield Offsetting:** The trading fees generated by this liquidity are harvested by the protocol to subsidize the treasury's capital costs, resulting in a net **0.0% interest rate** for the trader.

### 1.4 Treasury-Assisted Settlement
*   **The V4 Deadlock:** V4 requires all deltas to be settled within a single transaction.
*   **The Solution:** The `EswapRouter` pulls the required "bridge" capital from the protocol's `insuranceFund` to settle the physical delta with the `PoolManager`. The trader's debt is then tracked protocol-side, effectively allowing the position to stay open across multiple blocks/days.

## 2. Business Playbook (User Acquisition)

### 2.1 Aggregator & Solver Integration
ESWAP is designed for "Invisible Distribution." It is a back-end liquidity layer that aggregators route into automatically.

*   **URC-4 (Active Liquidity):** Implements `swapToPrice` and `getIndicativeQuote`, allowing solvers (UniswapX, CoW Swap) to include ESWAP in their routing path.
*   **URC-2 (HookSwap Events):** Emits standardized events so that volume and price impact are correctly indexed by DexScreener, Dune, and 1inch.
*   **URC-3 (HookStats):** Provides a read-only view of swappable capacity, enabling split-fill planners to accurately simulate high-value trades.

### 2.2 Zero-Capital Launch (Self-Seeding)
The protocol includes a **0.5% Reserve Factor** (Treasure Fee) on all trades.
1.  **Phase 1 (Bootstrap):** Founders launch with $0. The protocol facilitates 1x "leveraged" (spot) trades.
2.  **Phase 2 (Growth):** Fees from Phase 1 accumulate in the `insuranceFund`, which then serves as the bridge for 2x, 3x, and 5x leverage.
3.  **Phase 3 (Scale):** High volume from aggregators drives exponential treasury growth, allowing for effectively unlimited open interest.

### 2.3 Strategic Distribution
*   **B2B Launchpads:** Any token launched through an ESWAP-partnered launchpad automatically gets a 0% interest margin pool.
*   **Consumer Apps:** Standardized URC interfaces allow "1-Click Long/Short" buttons to be embedded in analytics dashboards (DexTools) or mobile wallets.

## 3. Risk Management & Liquidations
*   **Oracle-Free:** Liquidation checks occur natively within the `afterSwap` callback of the V4 pipeline, utilizing the `sqrtPriceX96` of the pool itself.
*   **Atomic:** If a swap pushes the price past the liquidation threshold, the hook atomically closes the position, swaps the collateral back to the debt asset, and returns the balance to the trader in the same block.
